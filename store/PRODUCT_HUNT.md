## Maker’s first comment

Draft. Add a line or two of your own story at the top; it’s the part people respond to.

Hi Product Hunt, I’m Kristina.

Buying jeans online usually means guessing. Every brand cuts differently, and the useful information, like “no stretch” or “runs small, size up”, is buried in the product details.

So I built Sizer. You tell it your measurements, or a few things you own and how they fit. On a product page, it reads the brand’s size chart, the fabric and the fit notes, and puts your size right under the size picker.

A few things I cared about:
• It shows its working. Every answer has a plain-English reason, like “No stretch, sized up”.
• It’s honest when it’s unsure. Thin information gets a “rough guess” label and a note on what would firm it up.
• If your size is sold out, it says which sizes are in stock and how far off they are.
• Your measurements never leave your browser. No account, no servers, no tracking.

It’s strongest for women’s jeans, trousers and skirts today, with charts for 40 brands. I’d love to know which brands and shops to add next.
